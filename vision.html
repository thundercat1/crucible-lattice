Product Vision

From structure to supply chain, in seconds.

A chemist submits a molecule. Crucible Lattice returns a Pareto-optimal set of manufacturing options — scored across cost, speed, risk, and sustainability, filtered through the regulatory surface that use case actually faces, drawn from a live graph of 2,413 qualified partners.

01

A capability graph, not a directory.

Every partner in the network is indexed by what they can actually make — tooling, certifications, scale bands, regulatory coverage. Matching is structural, not keyword-based. Morgan fingerprints against partner capability vectors, not free-text search.

02

AI-native retrosynthesis and optimization.

The system proposes alternative routes when the submitted structure is suboptimal — lower yield, REACH-flagged solvents, higher E-factor. Optimization potential surfaces alongside raw feasibility, not as an afterthought.

03

A learning flywheel.

Every transaction tightens the cost, lead-time, and risk estimates for the next one. The lattice gets faster, cheaper, and more accurate the more it runs. This is a structural advantage that compounds — not a feature competitors can copy in a quarter.

What's different

Specialty chemistry procurement today is months. We compress it to hours.

A relationship-based, manual procurement process takes weeks of emails and calls before a single quote arrives. Crucible Lattice runs the analysis in seconds — simultaneously enforcing ITAR gates, REACH compliance, genotoxic-impurity screens, and Pareto-optimal partner selection across a network too large for any one person to hold in their head.
